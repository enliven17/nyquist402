## The basic payment flow

Basic auth-capture authorizes a maximum spending amount and lets the seller capture the actual cost. It uses a separate contract from protected settlement. The seller consents to the final amount; there is no claim review or arbitration process.

## Settlement model

The seller consents to capture from an authorized hold. A basic payment settles under its capture rules; protected claim review and arbitration do not apply to it. Use the [protected USDC demo](/demo) to explore the review-based flow.

## Capture, release and refund

Capture pays from the escrow hold. Void releases unused held funds. A refund requires a funder to supply the returned tokens: it cannot claw back funds from the seller after payment. The payer can reclaim an eligible unused hold after the capture deadline.

## Choose the appropriate protection

Use [protected settlement](/docs/protected/lifecycle) when individual deliveries need buyer review and claim disputes. An existing basic payment does not gain those rules automatically.
