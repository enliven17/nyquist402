## Contract operations

| Operation | Authority | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `authorize(payment_info)` | Operator and payer | Hold the maximum in escrow |
| `charge(payment_info, amount, fee_amount, fee_receiver)` | Operator, payer and seller consent | Pay immediately and return the remainder |
| `capture(payment_info, amount, fee_amount, fee_receiver, void_remainder)` | Operator and seller consent | Pay from the held budget |
| `void(payment_info)` | Operator and seller consent | Release the remaining hold |
| `refund(payment_info, amount, funder)` | Operator, seller consent and funder | Return tokens supplied by the funder |
| `reclaim(payment_info)` | Payer | Recover eligible held funds after the capture deadline |

## Consent boundaries

Consent comes from `receiver_authorizer`, or `receiver` when unset, and covers exact call arguments. The escrow rejects the operator as consent address or refund funder. A facilitator cannot independently choose a seller's payout.

## Refund funding

The refund funder supplies returned tokens. An insurer, treasury or seller may fund the refund with the required consent. This is different from a protected ruling, which distributes the disputed claim's retained funds.
