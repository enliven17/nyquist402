## Prepare your wallet

Use a supported Stellar wallet on **Testnet** with test XLM for reserves and transaction fees.
Open [/demo](/demo), connect your wallet, and sign the service sign-in challenge. The challenge authenticates access to private history and evidence; it is never submitted to the network.

Choose **Add USDC trustline**, review and sign that transaction, then open [Circle's faucet](https://faucet.circle.com/). Select **USDC / Stellar Testnet** and paste the connected wallet address. Complete any faucet verification yourself, then return to the demo; the balance refreshes automatically.

The asset is USDC issued by `GBBD47IF6LWK7P7MDEVSCWR7DPUWV3NY3DTQEVFL4NAT4AQH3ZLLFLA5`, as listed in [Circle's official addresses](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/usdc-contract-addresses). Stellar USDC uses **7 decimals**. The app never creates an issuer or mints a demo currency.

## Buyer: agree and fund

Choose the **Buyer** view. Review the agreement, budget, selected arbiter, deadlines, fallback and any separate fee reserves. Select **Review terms**, then sign the exact total. The existing v1 deployment is fee-free; fee-capable v2 requires its own configured deployment.

A default 10 USDC work budget starts with 10 available and none reserved. Keep the payment ID. A saved offer or pending transaction is not proof of funding.

## Seller: record a delivery

Switch to **Seller**. Record a 3 USDC delivery with its description. The body is stored privately before its hash is submitted. The ledger shows 7 available and 3 reserved; recording a claim does not pay the seller.

Demo seller controls are delegated only to the holder of this payment's capability. Production sellers use their own signer.

## Buyer: accept or dispute

Switch to **Buyer** and accept the exact 3 USDC claim. Once confirmed, 3 is paid and 7 remains available.

For a dispute, record a second 4 USDC delivery in Seller view. In Buyer view, enter evidence and dispute before review closes. Both sides can respond until the evidence deadline; previous body versions are retained.

## Arbiter: review and rule

Choose **Arbiter**. Load private case material and compare the bodies with the current onchain commitments and revision. After evidence closes, the currently assigned arbiter can issue a bounded award. Demo-operated arbiter buttons require a payment-party sign-in and the payment capability; an external arbiter signs with its own wallet.

Custom timeout passes authority to the pinned default; final timeout permits the agreed fallback.

## Return unused funds and recover

After session expiry, the buyer can reclaim the unused 3 USDC while the disputed 4 remains reserved. Eligible direct actions remain available when the optional worker is disabled.

Reconnect and sign in to recover server-side history from another browser. Importing a payment ID permits public state reads but grants no private evidence or signing authority. Reconcile pending transaction hashes before retrying.
