## Select the authority

Matching nonempty buyer and seller preferences select a mutually agreed custom arbiter. Otherwise the pinned default handles the case. The selected path, default identity, time windows and terminal fallback are part of the funded agreement.

After funding, a party or service cannot replace those terms unilaterally. Any different selection policy must be explicitly agreed before funding.

## Obtain and accept pricing

A paid arbiter authenticates a quote before funding. The buyer and seller verify that the custom and default quotes apply to the complete payment and arbitration policy, including the applicable fee limits. Zero-cost arbitration can omit a quote.

| Binding | Validation purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Arbiter and fee recipient | Identify who is authorized to rule and receive the accepted fee |
| Payment / case scope | Prevent a quote being applied to unrelated work or liability |
| Asset, network and deployment | Prevent cross-asset or cross-deployment interpretation |
| Price and limits | Bound per-case and total arbitration liability |
| Validity and reuse conditions | Reject expired, altered, stale or improperly reused quotes |
| Evidence and deadline policy | Ensure the offered service matches the funded dispute process |

Both parties accept the arbitration terms; the buyer authorizes the funded fee amounts and caps. A quote response must not be treated as trusted merely because it came through the facilitator.

## Escalation

The agreement must account for default handling when a custom arbiter times out. Handoff preserves evidence access, the current revision and existing deadlines. It must not create a new fee obligation outside the buyer's accepted limit.

## Quote encoding

The contract provides `register_quote(FeeQuote)`, authenticated by the named arbiter. The quote contains `scope`, `arbiter`, `fee`, `valid_until` and `salt`; its ID is the SHA-256 of its canonical Soroban XDR. Its scope hashes the network, deployment and complete payment terms with both quote IDs cleared, using a dedicated quote-scope domain to separate quotes from other signed data.

Funding references the accepted quote IDs. The contract verifies authority, scope, exact fee and validity through the funding deadline. Changing parties, budget, policy, token, deployment or payment salt invalidates applicability. Registration is a separate arbiter-signed transaction; it is not a trust assertion from the facilitator. Zero-cost arbitration can omit a quote.

## Quote discovery — not fully implemented yet

Arbitration integrations will expose published pricing and direct quotes through the protected payment negotiation flow. The client will check each returned quote against the agreed terms before signing; receiving a quote through a facilitator will not replace arbiter authentication. Public HTTP schemas will be defined with the [protected x402 integration](/docs/integration).
