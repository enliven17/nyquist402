## Advertise supported terms

The resource server advertises its protected capability, network, deployment, asset and delivery/pricing criteria through x402 negotiation. Agree the buyer's exact budget, review policy, arbiter selection, accepted quote references and fallback before funding.

A seller must not substitute basic settlement when a buyer required protection.

## Execute and record delivery

After confirmed funding, the seller runs the service and creates a usage/delivery receipt that can be checked against the agreed pricing criteria. The receipt and delivery body remain retrievable offchain; their commitments bind the onchain claim.

Record each claim within the available principal and reserved arbitration capacity before the session deadline. Claims have independent review periods. Recording delivery does not authorize unrestricted capture of the budget.

## Handle review and disputes

The seller interface shows active claims, evidence requests, response deadlines, accepted arbitration terms and settlement status. The seller can initiate a claim dispute or answer the buyer in the existing case without a separate deposit.

Retain delivery and usage evidence and deliver it through the case service. A ruling must bind the current evidence revision.

## Reconcile payments

Track accepted claims, undisputed settlement, arbitration awards and unused-fund returns against the ledger. Already finalized claims remain final when another claim is disputed. Returning unused principal cannot consume a pending claim's reservation.

## Signing policy

Production sellers control their own signing credentials and permitted actions. Payment-scoped seller controls in the current demo are demonstration delegation, not the intended production authentication model. See [signing](/docs/signing).
