## Evaluate an offer

Require explicit protected-payment capability from the seller and facilitator. Verify the service agreement, pricing criteria, asset, principal budget, deployment, deadlines, arbiter policy and fallback allocation. An agent requiring protection must reject a basic-only offer before signing.

## Review and fund

The buyer interface presents the work budget, protocol-fee reserve, arbitration reserve, accepted quotes and per-case/total caps separately. The buyer signs the exact versioned terms after the seller agrees. An agent applies the same checks through the SDK rather than relying on interface text.

Funding establishes the permitted budget and policy; it does not pre-approve every later delivery or give a service unrestricted spending authority.

## Review delivery claims

Read the delivery receipt, usage record, claim amount and review deadline. Accepting a claim commits to that exact delivery claim. If review expires without a dispute, the claim becomes eligible for settlement.

To dispute, retain the evidence body, submit its commitment before review closes and make the body available through the authorized case service. Update evidence within the response window when needed.

## Track and recover

The buyer interface exposes payment history, pending transaction hashes, claim states, evidence access and deadline alerts. On reload or a different browser, authenticate with the buyer account and recover service history reconciled with onchain state.

After session expiry, reclaim unused principal and eligible reserve returns. Pending claims retain the funds they need. Confirm the transaction outcome before retrying a mutation.
