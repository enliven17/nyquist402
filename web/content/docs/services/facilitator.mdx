**The public facilitator API and managed execution are not fully implemented yet.** The [public API contract](/docs/reference/service-api) and [OpenAPI schemas](/service-contract.openapi.json) define how they will work; the endpoint descriptions below are target behavior.

## Discover and pin capabilities

`GET /supported` identifies the service origin, network/passphrase, contract and code hash, token and decimals, pinned default arbiter, supported credentials, policy names and limits. Clients compare this record with the seller's requirements before preparing signatures. A service advertising only basic capture cannot satisfy protected review and arbitration.

Capabilities can be cached for 300 seconds. A funded payment retains its original scope even if a subsequent capability record changes. The record does not grant a relay permission to sign for a buyer, seller or arbiter.

## Verify without broadcasting

`POST /verify` checks complete terms and a canonical payment ID against either signed funding XDR or an existing funding transaction hash. It returns `valid`, `fundingStatus`, `checkedAtLedger` and, on rejection, a machine-readable reason. It makes no transfer and does not treat a prepared or merely submitted transaction as funding.

Verification includes exact token movements, required signatures and invocation trees, accepted quote applicability and economic limits. A proof of successful funding is checked against the deployed payment state. Unavailable chain data produces a retryable service error instead of an optimistic valid result.

## Submit an authorized action

The client obtains a prepared action, completes any additional authorizations and signs the source envelope. `POST /settle` accepts its `preparationId` and `signedXdr`, under a wallet session and `Idempotency-Key`. It verifies that the signed body is the prepared body, persists the transaction hash and exact XDR, and only then broadcasts.

A transaction still awaiting confirmation returns `202 pending` and a `statusUrl`; confirmed success returns `200 confirmed`. `GET /transactions/{hash}` exposes the outcome without leaking private material or the signed envelope. A submitted dispute remains a dispute, and submitted funding remains funding: the endpoint name never implies immediate seller payment.

## Retry and recover safely

An identical idempotency key and request resolve to the same operation. A changed request under that key returns a conflict. A crash between broadcast and response is recovered by checking the original hash. While its outcome is unknown, workers may rebroadcast the same valid XDR but may not create a replacement payout.

Clients poll after 2 seconds and back off to a maximum 15 seconds, honoring `Retry-After`. A conclusively failed or expired transaction can lead to fresh preparation only after current state is reconciled. An RPC outage or a missing index result is not proof that a transaction expired. See the exact [transaction and error rules](/docs/reference/service-api#idempotency-and-interrupted-requests).

## Deadline execution

Workers check due actions every 5 seconds. They use a 30-second renewable lease on each claim/action, persist job identity before broadcast and re-read eligibility before submission. Only contract-eligible undisputed settlement or terminal fallback is submitted automatically. Acceptance, disputes, evidence and merits rulings require their appropriate role authority.

The managed target is first broadcast within 30 seconds at p95 when chain/RPC and the fee budget are available. This is a submission target, not a confirmation promise or a power to extend deadlines. Waiting deadlines, eligible work, leased work, pending transactions, unknown outcomes, confirmed actions and blocked jobs are distinct visible states. [Operations](/docs/services/operators) defines monitoring and incident response.

## Outages and direct execution

Parties may prepare and submit their permitted actions directly against the funded contract. Eligible callers may finalize undisputed claims and fallback without relay approval. Service unavailability cannot add a new veto to the funded agreement.

Before direct submission, reconcile any pending hash and preserve the original network, deployment and terms. Evidence delivery remains separately authenticated and follows its [retention policy](/docs/services/evidence#retention-and-confidentiality).
