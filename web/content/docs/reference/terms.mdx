## The funded agreement

Buyer and seller authorize the complete agreement before funding. The agreement binds the parties, payment asset, work budget, deadlines, arbiter selection, fallback and fee limits to one network and escrow contract.

| Scope | Bound information |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Buyer, seller, asset, network, deployment and payment uniqueness |
| Work | Agreement commitment, pricing/delivery criteria and principal budget |
| Timing | Funding/session limits, claim review, evidence response and ruling windows |
| Arbitration | Custom preferences, pinned default, accepted quotes and terminal fallback |
| Fees | Protocol and arbitration reserves, fee rates, recipients and per-case caps |

Protected HTTP negotiation is **not fully implemented yet**. Its wire schema will identify the contract and agreement format so the integration can verify compatibility before requesting signatures.

## Payment terms

| Fields | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `payer`, `seller`, `token` | Parties and payment asset |
| `agreement`, `max_amount`, `salt` | Agreement hash, base-unit work budget and 32-byte uniqueness value |
| `fund_by`, `delivery_deadline` | Funding and session deadlines |
| `buyer_arbiter`, `seller_arbiter`, `default_arbiter` | Custom preferences and pinned default |
| `review_seconds`, `response_seconds` | Review and evidence durations |
| `arbiter_seconds`, `default_seconds` | Ruling durations |
| `fallback_seller_bps` | Agreed terminal seller share in basis points |
| `protocol_reserve`, `arbitration_reserve` | Separately funded fee balances, additional to the work budget |
| `protocol_bps`, `protocol_recipient` | Accepted protocol fee rate and recipient |
| `custom_fee`, `default_fee`, `case_fee_cap` | Accepted arbitration charges and per-case maximum |
| `custom_quote`, `default_quote` | Applicable authenticated arbiter quote references |

The TypeScript client groups fee fields under `terms.fees` and flattens them for canonical onchain encoding. Amounts are integer base-unit strings. Hashes are 32-byte values represented as hex; deadlines use Unix seconds and durations use seconds.

## Total funding and fee limits

The payer authorizes principal plus protocol reserve plus arbitration reserve. Fee reserves do not reduce the work budget. The per-case cap and total funded arbitration reserve jointly bound arbitration exposure.

Zero-fee offers use the same agreement structure with zero charges and reserves. A zero-cost arbiter can omit its quote reference. See [funding and fee reserves](/docs/protected/fees) and [quotes](/docs/protected/quotes).

## Payment identity

Canonical encoding binds the full terms to the network and contract. Changing parties, work budget, fees, deadlines or arbitration terms changes the payment identity. A signature for one agreement cannot authorize a different one.

## Compatibility checks

The client verifies the selected network, contract and supported agreement format before signing. A funded payment keeps its original terms and deployment through settlement and recovery. Service defaults cannot change an existing agreement.

Basic auth-capture is a separate capability. An integration requiring protected review and arbitration must reject a basic-only offer rather than silently downgrade its payment.
